## Overview

Admins and Builders can create a playbook (workflow) from the Playbooks section of any vault they have access to. When you start a new playbook, Graphium AI asks you to describe what you want it to do. It then generates a complete draft including a name, a trigger, and an ordered set of steps. From there, you review and refine the draft in the playbook editor, connect the tables and tools each step needs, configure the trigger, and publish to make it active.

---

## Step 1: Open the Playbooks Section

From within your vault, select **Playbooks** in the left sidebar. If you do not see this option, confirm your vault role is **Admin** or **Builder** - Contributors and Viewers do not have access to Playbooks.

Click **+ New Playbook**.

## Step 2: Describe Your Playbook

A **Create Playbook** modal opens with two fields:

**AI Assistant**

A dropdown that controls which assistant this playbook is associated with. Selecting a specific assistant links the playbook to that assistant's context — the AI will be aware of that assistant's configured tables and tools when generating steps. Leave it set to **General** if the playbook is not tied to a specific assistant.

**What should this playbook do?**

A free-text field (up to 5,000 characters) where you describe what you want the playbook to do. Write in plain language — for example, *"Every morning, check for new patient records added in the last 24 hours and summarize the results."* The more specific you are, the better the output.

Click **Generate Playbook** once your description is ready.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/playbooks/create-playbook-modal.png" alt="Create Playbook modal showing AI Assistant dropdown and description field" />
</Frame>

### Tips for Writing Better Descriptions

The quality of the generated steps depends heavily on how clearly you describe the goal:

- **Include a time or trigger signal** if you want a scheduled playbook — phrases like "every Monday", "daily at 9am", or "each week" will produce a Scheduled trigger.
- **Name the data you're working with** — mentioning table names like "Patients" or "Providers" helps the AI reference the right data in its step instructions.
- **Describe the outcome, not just the process** — "send each patient a reminder 7 days before their appointment" is more useful than "check appointments and send messages".
- **Include any conditions** — "only for patients with a status of Active" or "if no provider is assigned" will be reflected in the generated step instructions.

## What the AI Generates

After a few seconds, the editor opens with a fully drafted playbook. The AI produces:

- **A playbook name** derived from your description
- **A trigger type** inferred from your description — for example, a description mentioning "every week" or "every morning" will produce a Scheduled trigger
- **An ordered set of steps**, each with a title and written instructions explaining what the AI should do at that step

The playbook opens in the editor as **v1 · Draft**, ready to review and refine.

## Step 3: Understanding the Playbook Editor

The playbook editor is organized into three areas:

- **Playbook Settings (left sidebar)** — The assistant running the playbook, a description, **Playbook Access** (shared tables, connections, and tools), and a **Runs** panel showing this playbook's run history.
- **Flow canvas (center)** — A vertical, top-to-bottom flow showing the trigger card followed by each numbered step card, in the order they run. Click any card to configure it.
- **Configuration panel (right)** — Shows the settings for whatever you've selected in the canvas or sidebar: **Trigger Configuration** when the trigger card is selected, **Step Details** when a step card is selected, or **Playbook Access** when you click **Configure shared access**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/playbooks/playbook-editor-layout.png" alt="Playbook editor showing the left sidebar, center flow canvas, and right configuration panel" />
</Frame>

## Step 4: Review and Edit Your Steps

The AI generates a step title and written instructions for each step, but does not automatically wire up data context. After generation, each step's resources are governed by whatever you grant in Playbook Access — see Step 6 below for configuring shared access.

Click a step card in the canvas to open its **Step Details** in the right panel. Each step has:

- **Step Title** — A short label that identifies the step (for example, *Query Today's Case Data*).
- **Instructions** — The detailed, plain-language instructions that tell the AI exactly what to do during this step.
- **Advanced Options** — An expandable section containing **Success Criteria**, where you can define a condition and choose what happens if it isn't met (**Fail Playbook**, **Stop Without Failure**, or **Require Review**).

Review the generated instructions carefully and edit any details the AI may have assumed — field names, filter conditions, or specific values that should match your vault's actual data.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/playbooks/playbook-step-details.png" alt="Step Details panel showing Step Title, Instructions, and Advanced Options" />
</Frame>

> 💡 **Tip:** Write instructions in plain language and be specific. Reference exact table names and field values where helpful — for example: *"From the Cases table, select all rows where case_date = today and status is Completed or Signed."*

To manage steps in the canvas:

- **Add a step** — Click the **+** icon between any two cards (or at the end of the flow) to insert a new step at that point.
- **Reorder, duplicate, or delete a step** — Click the **⋮** menu on a step card for **Move up**, **Move down**, **Duplicate step**, or **Delete Step**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/playbooks/manage-playbook-steps.png" alt="Step card showing the insert (+) control and the ⋮ menu with Move up, Move down, Duplicate step, and Delete Step" />
</Frame>

> **Note:** Steps run in sequence, top to bottom. The next step only starts after the previous one finishes, and output from earlier steps is available to later ones.

## Step 5: Configure the Trigger

Click the **trigger card** at the top of the flow canvas to open **Trigger Configuration** in the right panel.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/playbooks/add-playbook-trigger.png" alt="Trigger card at the top of the flow canvas" />
</Frame>

Use the **Run when** dropdown to search and select a trigger type, grouped by category:

- **General** — **Manual** (run manually from the dashboard) or **Scheduled** (run on a recurring schedule).
- **Vault Events** — **Record Added**, **Record Updated**, **Record Deleted** — fire automatically when a record changes in a specific vault table.
- **Connection events** (listed per connection, e.g., New Row Added from Google Sheets) — fire automatically when something happens in a connected external system.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/playbooks/select-playbook-trigger.png" alt="Run when dropdown showing General, Vault Events, and connection-specific trigger options" />
</Frame>

Selecting a trigger type reveals its specific fields directly in the same panel — for example, choosing **Scheduled** reveals **Frequency**, **Time**, and **Time Zone** fields. For Vault Event and connection triggers, see [Triggering Playbooks from Connections and Vault Events](/graphium/playbooks/triggering-playbooks-from-connections) for full setup details.

You can also add an optional **Description** to the trigger to document when and why the playbook runs. Click **Clear all** to reset the trigger configuration.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/playbooks/schedule-playbook-trigger.png" alt="Trigger Configuration panel showing Frequency, Time, Time Zone, and Description fields for a Scheduled trigger" />
</Frame>

## Step 6: Configure Shared Access

In the left sidebar, click **Configure shared access** under **Playbook Access** to open the resource panel. This is where you grant the playbook access to the vault resources it needs — the tables, connections, and tools available to every step.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/playbooks/add-playbook-context.png" alt="Configure shared access button under Playbook Access in the left sidebar" />
</Frame>

The Playbook Access panel is organized into three sections:

**Tables** — Toggle on any vault tables the playbook needs to read from or write to. Each table shows its permission level (**Read** or **Read/Write**) and its approval mode (**Autonomous** or **Requires Review**). Setting a table to **Requires Review** causes the run to pause before any write action is taken on that table.

**Connections** — Toggle on any vault connections the playbook needs to use. Like tables, each connection has an approval mode. Set a connection to **Requires Review** if you want the run to pause before sending data to an external system.

**Tools** — Toggle on any built-in tools the playbook may use during execution, such as Chart Generation or Web Search. Tools also have an Autonomous/Requires Review approval mode.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/playbooks/playbook-context-panel.png" alt="Playbook Access panel showing Tables, Connections, and Tools sections" />
</Frame>

> 💡 **Tip:** Start with the minimum access your workflow needs. You can always add more resources later by editing the playbook and publishing a new version.

---

## Step 7: Save as Draft

Click **Save** in the top-right header to save your current configuration as a draft version. The header shows the current version number and draft status (for example, *v1 · Draft*), along with a **Ready to publish** badge once the playbook has at least one configured step.

Drafts are not active — saving does not run or publish the playbook. You can save multiple times as you refine the playbook.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/playbooks/save-draft-playbook.png" alt="Save button in the playbook editor header" />
</Frame>

---

## Step 8: Publish the Playbook

When you are ready to make the playbook active, click **Publish** in the top-right header. This promotes your current draft to the active published version.

Once published:

- The playbook shows an **Enabled** badge in the list and in the editor header.
- Manual playbooks are immediately available to run.
- Scheduled playbooks begin running at the next configured time.
- Connection and Vault Event playbooks are automatically deployed — the trigger is registered with the external service and begins listening for events immediately.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/playbooks/publish-playbook.png" alt="Publish button and Enabled badge" />
</Frame>

> **Note:** Only Builders and Admins can publish a playbook. At least one step with instructions must be configured before publishing.

Once published, you can edit, version, and disable your playbook. See [Managing Your Playbooks](/graphium/playbooks/managing-your-playbooks) for details.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely Cause | What to Do |
|-------|--------------|------------|
| Playbooks does not appear in the sidebar | Your vault role is Contributor or Viewer | Ask your vault Admin or Builder to adjust your role |
| Publish button is not available | No steps have been configured, or no instructions have been added | Add at least one step with instructions before publishing |
| A table or connection is not available in Playbook Access | The resource has not been added to this vault | Navigate to the Tables or Connections section and add it first |
