## Overview

Each vault can have multiple AI Assistants configured — each with a different focus, data access, and set of capabilities. You can switch between assistants at any time using the assistant selector above the chat input bar, or browse all available assistants from the **Assistants** page in the vault sidebar.

## When to Use This

Use this when you want to ask a question better suited to a specialized assistant — for example, switching to a Data Analyst for in-depth reporting, or an Operations Helper for workflow questions.

## Prerequisites

- The vault must have at least one AI Assistant configured
- You must have access to the vault

### Switch from the Chat Input Bar

1. Open a vault. The Home page loads with the chat input bar visible.
2. Click the **assistant selector** — a labeled button showing the current assistant's name (for example, **GENERAL ASSISTANT ⌄**) above the input bar, or in the input bar's toolbar once a conversation has started. A popup appears listing each available assistant with a short description, with a checkmark next to the currently active one.
3. Click the assistant you want to use. The selector updates to reflect the selection.
4. Type your question and send it.

The picker also includes a **Manage assistants** link at the bottom, which opens the **Assistants** page.

### Access Assistants from the Sidebar

1. In the vault sidebar, click the **Assistants** link below the Tables and Chats shortcuts (scroll down if your table or chat list is long) to access the Custom Assistants.
2. Each card shows the assistant's name, description, and the number of tables and connections it has access to.
3. Click an assistant card to start a new conversation with it.
4. To create, edit, or delete an assistant, see [Managing Your AI Assistants](/graphium/ai-assistants/managing-your-ai-assistants) (requires Admin or Builder role).

### The General Assistant

Every vault includes a **General** assistant by default. The General assistant has no specialized focus and can answer questions across whatever tables, tools, and connections are enabled in Chat Context. Use it when your question doesn't fit any of the specialized assistants, or when you're starting a quick one-off conversation.

### Example Specialized Assistants

Vaults can be configured with any number of specialized assistants. Common examples include:

| Assistant | Focus |
|-----------|-------|
| **Data Analyst** | In-depth data analysis, reporting, and metrics |
| **Operations Helper** | Operational workflows and process questions |
| **Compliance Monitor** | Compliance checks and regulatory monitoring |

The exact assistants in your vault depend on what your team has configured.

## What to Expect

Each assistant may have different table access, tools, and connection permissions. Switching assistants starts a new conversation with the selected one.

## Common Issues & Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Only the General assistant appears in the selector | No specialized assistants are configured for this vault | Open the **Assistants** page and create one — see [Managing Your AI Assistants](/graphium/ai-assistants/managing-your-ai-assistants), or contact your vault Admin |
| **New Assistant** button is missing | Your vault role does not permit assistant creation | Ask your vault Admin to grant Admin or Builder access |
| Can't find the Assistants page | Your table or chat list may be pushing the Assistants link below the visible area | Scroll down within the sidebar, or click the collapse arrow at the bottom to switch to the icon rail and click the Assistants icon |
| An assistant that should be available doesn't appear in the selector | The assistant may not be published, or may be restricted to specific vault roles | Check the assistant's status and **Who Can Access** setting in [Managing Your AI Assistants](/graphium/ai-assistants/managing-your-ai-assistants) |
