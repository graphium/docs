## Overview

Each vault can have multiple AI Assistants configured — each with a different focus, data access, and set of capabilities. You can switch between assistants at any time using the assistant selector, the **ASSISTANTS** section of the vault sidebar, or the **Assistants** tab of the workspace home.

## When to Use This

Use this when you want to ask a question better suited to a specialized assistant — for example, switching to a Data Analyst for in-depth reporting, or an Operations Helper for workflow questions.

## Prerequisites

- The vault must have at least one AI Assistant configured
- You must have access to the vault

### Switch from the Chat Input Bar

1. Open a vault. The Home page loads with the chat input bar visible.
2. Click the **assistant selector** — a labeled button showing the current assistant's name (for example, **GENERAL ASSISTANT ⌄**) above the heading, or in the input bar's toolbar once a conversation has started. A popup appears listing each available assistant with a short description, with a checkmark next to the currently active one.
3. Click the assistant you want to use. The selector updates to reflect the selection.
4. Type your question and send it.

The picker also includes a **Manage assistants** link at the bottom, which opens the **Custom Assistants** page.

### Switch from the Sidebar

The **ASSISTANTS** section of the vault sidebar lists the **General Assistant** first, followed by the vault's published custom assistants. If there are more than five, click **[N] More** to see the rest.

1. Click an assistant in the **ASSISTANTS** section. A new chat with that assistant opens on the Home page.
2. Type your question and send it.

Vault Admins also see two buttons next to the **ASSISTANTS** heading:

- **⚙ (Manage assistants)** — opens the **Custom Assistants** page, where you can create, edit, and archive assistants. See [Managing Your AI Assistants](/graphium/ai-assistants/managing-your-ai-assistants).
- **+ (Add assistant)** — creates a new assistant. See [Creating an AI Assistant](/graphium/ai-assistants/creating-an-ai-assistant).

Builders don't see these buttons. To manage or add assistants, use the **Manage assistants** link at the bottom of the assistant selector.

### Switch from the Workspace Home

The **Assistants** tab of the workspace home lists the custom assistants you can use across all your vaults. Click an assistant's card or **Chat** to open a new chat with it in its vault. See [Browsing Assistants in Your Workspace](/graphium/ai-assistants/browsing-assistants-in-your-workspace).

### Chatting with a Selected Assistant

When a custom assistant is selected, the Home page is set up for that assistant:

- The assistant's name appears in the selector above the heading, and the chat input reads *"Ask [assistant name]…"*
- The description under the heading is the assistant's own description
- **RECENT CHATS WITH [assistant name]** shows only your conversations with that assistant

To go back to the General Assistant, pick **General Assistant** in the selector or in the sidebar.

### The General Assistant

Every vault includes a **General** assistant by default. The General assistant has no specialized focus and can answer questions across whatever tables, tools, and connections are enabled in Chat Context. Use it when your question doesn't fit any of the specialized assistants, or when you're starting a quick one-off conversation.

### Example Specialized Assistants

Vaults can be configured with any number of specialized assistants. Common examples include:

| Assistant | Focus |
|-----------|-------|
| **Data Analyst** | In-depth data analysis, reporting, and metrics |
| **Operations Helper** | Operational workflows and process questions |
| **Compliance Monitor** | Compliance checks and regulatory monitoring |

The exact assistants in your vault depend on what your team has configured.

## What to Expect

Each assistant may have different table access, tools, and connection permissions. Selecting an assistant — from the selector, the sidebar, or the workspace home — always starts a new conversation with it. Your earlier conversations stay in the **CHATS** list.

If an assistant is archived, its conversations stay readable but are labeled **Inactive** and can't be continued. See [Chats with an Archived Assistant](/graphium/ai-assistants/the-ai-assistant-welcome-screen#chats-with-an-archived-assistant).

## Common Issues & Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Only the General assistant appears in the selector | No specialized assistants are configured for this vault | Admins can click **+** next to **ASSISTANTS** in the sidebar, and Builders can use **Manage assistants** in the selector, to create one — see [Creating an AI Assistant](/graphium/ai-assistants/creating-an-ai-assistant) — or contact your vault Admin |
| The ⚙ and **+** buttons next to ASSISTANTS are missing | These buttons are for vault Admins only | Builders can use **Manage assistants** in the assistant selector. Contributors and Viewers should ask a vault Admin for Builder access |
| My previous conversation disappeared when I picked an assistant | Picking an assistant always starts a new chat | Reopen the earlier conversation from the **CHATS** list in the sidebar |
| I can't type in a chat labeled **Inactive** | The assistant has been archived | Start a new chat with an active assistant |
| An assistant that should be available doesn't appear in the selector | The assistant may not be published, or may be restricted to specific vault roles | Check the assistant's status and **Who Can Access** setting in [Managing Your AI Assistants](/graphium/ai-assistants/managing-your-ai-assistants) |
