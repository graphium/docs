## Overview

When you open a vault in Graphium AI, the **Home** page is your AI-powered starting point. It displays your recent conversations, suggested prompts, and a chat input bar — giving you immediate access to ask questions about your vault data. The Home page also provides access to your full conversation history across past sessions.

Use the Home page as your starting point whenever you want to ask questions about your vault data, request summaries, explore insights using AI, or return to a previous conversation. You must be a vault member with at least Contributor access, and the vault must have at least one AI Assistant configured.

---

## Opening the Vault

From the workspace dashboard, click on any vault to open it. The **Home** page loads automatically as the default view.

---

## The Vault Home Page

The Home page displays:

- An **assistant selector** above the chat input showing the currently active assistant (e.g., **GENERAL ASSISTANT ⌄**) — click it to switch to a different configured assistant
- A **chat input bar** with the placeholder *"Ask a question about your data..."*
- A **TRY ASKING** section with suggested prompts relevant to your vault data
- A **RECENT CHATS** section showing your four most recent conversations as cards — each showing the conversation title, which assistant was used, and how long ago it was

## The Vault Sidebar

The vault sidebar, to the left of the Home page, has two states, toggled by the arrow icon at the bottom.

**Expanded (default)** shows quick-access shortcuts for the current vault:

- **Ask Graphium** — returns to the AI Assistant home page
- **TABLES** — pinned tables in the vault, with a **+** to add one and **[N] More** to see the rest
- **CHATS** — your recent conversations, with a **+** to start a new one
- Below Tables and Chats, labeled links for **Assistants**, **Playbooks**, **Connections**, and **Settings** — scroll down in the sidebar if your table or chat list is long enough to push these out of view
- A **Builder / Contributor** toggle at the bottom to switch between roles you have access to

**Collapsed** shrinks the sidebar to an icon-only rail, showing the same sections in a more compact form: an icon for the Home page, one icon per pinned table, then **Assistants** (briefcase icon), **Playbooks** (book icon), **Connections** (link icon), and **Settings** (gear icon).

## Starting a New Conversation

You have a few options for starting a fresh conversation:

- Click the **chat input bar** and type your question
- Click one of the **TRY ASKING** suggestions to start with a pre-built prompt
- Click the **assistant selector** above the input bar to pick a specific assistant before you start typing

New conversations default to the **General** assistant unless you pick a specialized assistant first.

---

## Accessing Conversation History

Your conversation history is accessible in two ways.

**From the Home page**

The **RECENT CHATS** section shows your four most recent conversations as cards. Each card shows the conversation title, the assistant used, and a relative timestamp (e.g., *5m ago*, *Yesterday*). Click any card to reopen that conversation.

**From the sidebar**

In the vault sidebar, the **CHATS** section lists your most recent conversations. Click **More** to expand the list. Click any conversation title to reopen it.

---

## What to Expect

Reopening a conversation allows you to continue asking follow-up questions — the AI retains the context of the previous messages. The vault home page is always accessible by clicking **Ask Graphium** in the vault sidebar.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| No recent chats appear on Home | No conversations have been started yet | Start a new conversation by typing in the chat input bar |
| Only the General assistant appears in the selector | No specialized assistants are configured for this vault | Open the **Assistants** section in the sidebar and configure one |
| Home is not visible in the sidebar | The vault may not have the AI feature enabled | Contact your vault Admin |
| A past conversation is not in Recent Chats | It may be older than the 4 most recent | Click the clock icon to access the full time-grouped history |
| CHATS section in the sidebar is empty | No conversations have been started in this vault | Start a new conversation from the Home page |
| Can't find Assistants, Playbooks, Connections, or Settings in the sidebar | Your table or chat list may be pushing these links below the visible area | Scroll down within the sidebar, or click the collapse arrow at the bottom to switch to the more compact icon rail |
