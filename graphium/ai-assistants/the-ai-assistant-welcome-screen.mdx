## Overview

Graphium AI gives you two places to start a conversation with an AI Assistant:

- The **Assistants** tab of the workspace home, which lists the custom assistants you can use across every vault
- The **Home** page of a vault, with the vault's chat input, suggested prompts, and your recent conversations

This article covers both, along with choosing the right assistant and returning to an earlier conversation. For sending messages and reading replies, see [Chatting with Your AI Assistant](/graphium/ai-assistants/chatting-with-your-ai-assistant).

You must be a vault member with at least Contributor access to chat in a vault.

---

## Starting from the Workspace Home

When you sign in — or click the **Graphium** logo in the top-left corner — the workspace home opens with two tabs:

- **Assistants** (the default) — the custom assistants you can use in this workspace
- **Vaults** — your vaults. See [Viewing and Opening Vaults](/graphium/vaults/viewing-and-opening-vaults).

The line under **Your Assistants** shows how many assistants you have and how many vaults they're spread across — for example, *3 assistants across 3 vaults*.

Each assistant card shows:

- The assistant's icon, name, and description
- The **vault** it works in
- How many **tables** and **connections** it can use
- A **Chat** link, and a ⚙ **Configure** button for vault Admins

Click a card or **Chat** to open the assistant's vault on the Home page with a new chat for that assistant.

Use the toggle in the top-right corner to switch between the card grid and a list view. The list view shows the same details in columns (Assistant, Vault, Tables, Connections, Actions), with **Chat** and ⚙ buttons on each row. Graphium AI remembers the view you chose.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/assistant/workspace-assistants-list.png" alt="Assistants tab in list view, with Assistant, Vault, Tables, Connections, and Actions columns and an Add an assistant button" />
</Frame>

A few more things you can do from the Assistants tab:

- **Add an assistant** — click the **Add an assistant** tile (*"Say what you need help with, and we'll set one up for you"*), or **+ Add an assistant** in list view. If you don't have any assistants yet, a **Create your first assistant** panel appears in place of the cards. See [Creating an AI Assistant](/graphium/ai-assistants/creating-an-ai-assistant).
- **Open an assistant's settings (vault Admins)** — click the ⚙ on its card or row. See [Managing Your AI Assistants](/graphium/ai-assistants/managing-your-ai-assistants).

> **Note:** The **General Assistant** isn't listed on the Assistants tab, and neither are archived or unpublished assistants. Every vault has a General Assistant — open a vault to chat with it.

## The Vault Home Page

To open a vault's Home page, click the **Vaults** tab of the workspace home and click any vault, or click **Home** at the top of the vault sidebar. The Home page opens with the **General Assistant** selected, unless you opened a specific assistant.

The Home page displays:

- An **assistant selector** above the heading showing the currently selected assistant (e.g., **GENERAL ASSISTANT ⌄**)
- A **chat input bar** with the selected assistant's name — for example, *"Ask General Assistant..."* or *"Ask Claims Denial Tracker..."*
- A **TRY ASKING** section with suggested prompts relevant to your vault data
- A **RECENT CHATS** section showing your four most recent conversations with the selected assistant

## Choosing an Assistant

Each vault can have several assistants, each with its own focus, data access, and tools.

- **The General Assistant** is part of every vault. It has no specialized focus and answers questions across whatever tables, tools, and connections are enabled in Chat Context. Use it for quick one-off questions, or when no specialized assistant fits.
- **Custom assistants** are set up by your team for specific jobs — for example, a *Claims Denial Tracker* or a *Provider Credentialing Tracker*.

### From the assistant selector

1. Click the **assistant selector** — above the heading, or in the input bar's toolbar once a conversation has started. A popup lists each available assistant with a short description, with a checkmark next to the current one.
2. Click the assistant you want to use.

The popup also has a **Manage assistants** link at the bottom (Admins and Builders), which opens the **Custom Assistants** page.

  <img src="/images/graphium/assistant/choose-assistant-icon.png" alt="Assistant selector popup showing available assistants with descriptions and a Manage assistants link" />
</Frame>

### From the sidebar

The **ASSISTANTS** section of the vault sidebar lists the **General Assistant** first, followed by the vault's published custom assistants. If there are more than five, click **[N] More** to see the rest. Click an assistant to open a new chat with it.

Vault Admins also see two buttons next to the **ASSISTANTS** heading:

- **⚙ (Manage assistants)** — opens the **Custom Assistants** page. See [Managing Your AI Assistants](/graphium/ai-assistants/managing-your-ai-assistants).
- **+ (Add assistant)** — creates a new assistant. See [Creating an AI Assistant](/graphium/ai-assistants/creating-an-ai-assistant).

Builders don't see these buttons — they use **Manage assistants** in the assistant selector instead.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/assistant/sidebar-assistants-section.png" alt="ASSISTANTS section of the vault sidebar listing the General Assistant and custom assistants, with the manage and add buttons" />
</Frame>

### When a custom assistant is selected

The Home page is set up for that assistant:

- Its name appears in the selector and in the chat input (*"Ask [assistant name]…"*)
- The description under the heading is the assistant's own description
- **RECENT CHATS WITH [assistant name]** shows only your conversations with that assistant

To go back to the General Assistant, pick **General Assistant** in the selector or in the sidebar.

## Starting a New Conversation

You can start a fresh conversation by:

- Typing in the **chat input bar**
- Clicking one of the **TRY ASKING** suggestions
- Picking an assistant in the **assistant selector**, the sidebar **ASSISTANTS** section, or the workspace **Assistants** tab — picking an assistant always opens a new chat
- Clicking **+** next to **CHATS** in the sidebar, which starts a new General Assistant chat

---

## Returning to a Previous Conversation

**From the Home page**

The **RECENT CHATS** section shows your four most recent conversations with the selected assistant as cards. Each card shows the conversation title, the assistant used, and a relative timestamp (e.g., *5m ago*, *Yesterday*). Click a card to reopen that conversation. To see conversations with a different assistant, select that assistant first.

**From the sidebar (Admins and Builders)**

The **CHATS** section lists your most recent conversations with every assistant, each with the icon of the assistant it was held with. Click **More** to expand the list, and click a conversation to reopen it. A chat that hasn't been given a title yet shows the assistant's name.

### Chats with an Archived Assistant

If an assistant is archived, your conversations with it are kept. In the **CHATS** list they're labeled **Inactive**. You can open and read them, but not continue them — the chat shows *"[Assistant name] is no longer active. Select an active assistant to continue chatting."* To carry on, start a new chat with an active assistant.

## What to Expect

- Each assistant may have different table access, tools, and connection permissions, so the same question can get different answers from different assistants.
- Picking an assistant always starts a new conversation. Your earlier conversations stay in **Recent Chats** and the **CHATS** list.
- Inside a vault, the back arrow (←) in the vault header returns you to the workspace home.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| The General Assistant isn't on the workspace Assistants tab | It's part of every vault, so it isn't listed separately | Open a vault from the **Vaults** tab and chat from its Home page |
| An assistant I expected isn't listed | It's archived, not yet published, restricted to other vault roles, or in a vault you can't access | Ask a vault Admin to check the assistant's status and **Who Can Access** setting, or to add you to the vault |
| Only the General assistant appears in the selector | No custom assistants are set up for this vault | Admins can click **+** next to **ASSISTANTS** in the sidebar, and Builders can use **Manage assistants** in the selector — see [Creating an AI Assistant](/graphium/ai-assistants/creating-an-ai-assistant) |
| The ⚙ and **+** next to ASSISTANTS, or the ⚙ on a workspace card, are missing | These buttons are for vault Admins only | Builders can use **Manage assistants** in the assistant selector. Contributors and Viewers should ask a vault Admin |
| The **Add an assistant** tile is missing | You can't create assistants in any vault and can't create vaults, or the workspace has reached its assistant limit | See [Creating an AI Assistant](/graphium/ai-assistants/creating-an-ai-assistant) |
| My previous conversation disappeared when I picked an assistant | Picking an assistant always starts a new chat | Reopen the earlier conversation from **Recent Chats** or the **CHATS** list |
| A past conversation isn't in Recent Chats | It's older than the 4 most recent, or it was with a different assistant | Select the assistant it was held with, or open it from the **CHATS** list |
| A chat is labeled **Inactive** and I can't type in it | The assistant it was held with has been archived | Read the chat as needed, then start a new chat with an active assistant |
| The CHATS list isn't in the sidebar | The sidebar is collapsed, or your vault role is Contributor or Viewer | Expand the sidebar with the arrow at the bottom. Contributors and Viewers can reopen chats from **Recent Chats** |
| I want to see my vaults | The workspace home opens on the Assistants tab | Click the **Vaults** tab |
