## Overview

When you open a vault in Graphium AI, the **Home** page is your AI-powered starting point. It displays your recent conversations, suggested prompts, and a chat input bar — giving you immediate access to ask questions about your vault data. The Home page also provides access to your full conversation history across past sessions.

Use the Home page as your starting point whenever you want to ask questions about your vault data, request summaries, explore insights using AI, or return to a previous conversation. You must be a vault member with at least Contributor access, and the vault must have at least one AI Assistant configured.

---

## The Vault Home Page

From the workspace home, click the **Vaults** tab and click any vault to open it. The Home page loads automatically as the default view, with the **General Assistant** selected. (If you open an assistant from the workspace **Assistants** tab instead, the Home page opens with that assistant selected — see [Browsing Assistants in Your Workspace](/graphium/ai-assistants/browsing-assistants-in-your-workspace).)

The Home page displays:

- An **assistant selector** above the heading showing the currently selected assistant (e.g., **GENERAL ASSISTANT ⌄**) — click it to switch to a different configured assistant
- A **chat input bar**. With the General Assistant, it reads *"Ask General Assistant..."*; with a custom assistant, it shows that assistant's name (for example, *"Ask Claims Denial Tracker..."*)
- A **TRY ASKING** section with suggested prompts relevant to your vault data
- A **RECENT CHATS** section showing your four most recent conversations with the selected assistant as cards — each showing the conversation title, which assistant was used, and how long ago it was. With a custom assistant selected, the heading reads **RECENT CHATS WITH [assistant name]**

## The Vault Sidebar

The vault sidebar, to the left of the Home page, has two states, toggled by the arrow icon at the bottom.

**Expanded (default)** shows these sections, in order:

- **Home** (house icon) — returns to the AI Assistant home page
- **ASSISTANTS** — the **General Assistant** first, then the vault's published custom assistants. Click one to start a new chat with it. Vault Admins also see a ⚙ (manage assistants) and a **+** (add an assistant) next to the heading
- **CHATS** — your recent conversations, each with the icon of the assistant it was held with. Click **+** to start a new General Assistant chat, and **[N] More** to see the rest
- **TABLES** — pinned tables in the vault, with a **+** to add one and **[N] More** to see the rest
- **VAULT** — **Playbooks**, **Connections**, and **Settings** (Admins and Builders)
- A **Builder / Contributor** toggle at the bottom to switch between roles you have access to

**Collapsed** shrinks the sidebar to an icon-only rail. The collapsed rail doesn't show your chats.

For the full sidebar reference, see [Navigating Your Vault Using the Sidebar](/graphium/vaults/navigating-your-vault-using-the-sidebar).

## Starting a New Conversation

You have a few options for starting a fresh conversation:

- Click the **chat input bar** and type your question
- Click one of the **TRY ASKING** suggestions to start with a pre-built prompt
- Click the **assistant selector** above the heading to pick a specific assistant before you start typing
- Click an assistant under **ASSISTANTS** in the sidebar — this always opens a new chat with that assistant
- Click **+** next to **CHATS** in the sidebar to start a new General Assistant chat

New conversations use the **General** assistant unless you pick a specialized assistant first.

## Accessing Conversation History

Your conversation history is accessible in two ways.

**From the Home page**

The **RECENT CHATS** section shows your four most recent conversations with the selected assistant as cards. Each card shows the conversation title, the assistant used, and a relative timestamp (e.g., *5m ago*, *Yesterday*). Click any card to reopen that conversation. To see conversations with a different assistant, select that assistant first.

**From the sidebar**

In the vault sidebar, the **CHATS** section (Admins and Builders) lists your most recent conversations with every assistant. Click **More** to expand the list. Click any conversation title to reopen it. A chat that hasn't been given a title yet shows the assistant's name.

## What to Expect

Reopening a conversation allows you to continue asking follow-up questions — the AI retains the context of the previous messages. The vault home page is always accessible by clicking **Home** in the vault sidebar.

  <img src="/images/graphium/assistant/ask-graphium.png" alt="Home link in the vault sidebar used to return to the AI Assistant home page" />

### Chats with an Archived Assistant

If an assistant is archived, your conversations with it are kept. In the **CHATS** list they're labeled **Inactive**. You can open and read them, but not continue them — the chat shows *"[Assistant name] is no longer active. Select an active assistant to continue chatting."* To carry on, start a new chat with an active assistant.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| No recent chats appear on Home | No conversations have been started yet | Start a new conversation by typing in the chat input bar |
| Only the General assistant appears in the selector | No specialized assistants are configured for this vault | Admins can click **+** next to **ASSISTANTS** in the sidebar, and Builders can use **Manage assistants** in the selector, to create one — see [Creating an AI Assistant](/graphium/ai-assistants/creating-an-ai-assistant) |
| Home is not visible in the sidebar | The vault may not have the AI feature enabled | Contact your vault Admin |
| A past conversation is not in Recent Chats | It may be older than the 4 most recent, or it was with a different assistant | Select the assistant it was held with, or open it from the **CHATS** list in the sidebar |
| CHATS section in the sidebar is empty | No conversations have been started in this vault | Start a new conversation from the Home page |
| A chat is labeled **Inactive** and I can't type in it | The assistant it was held with has been archived | Read the chat as needed, then start a new chat with an active assistant |
| Chats don't appear in the collapsed sidebar | The collapsed icon rail doesn't show chats | Expand the sidebar with the arrow at the bottom |
| Can't find Playbooks, Connections, or Settings in the sidebar | They're in the **VAULT** section at the bottom, which a long chat or table list can push out of view; or your role is Contributor or Viewer | Scroll down within the sidebar. If they still don't appear, ask a vault Admin about your role |
