## Overview

The **Billing** page gives you a single place to review the workspace's current subscription state — the active plan, billing period, seat counts, Graphium Credits balance, and the resource limits that apply to the workspace. Every workspace member can open the Billing page. Workspace **Members** see it read-only; only workspace **Owners** and **Admins** can change the plan, manage payment and invoices, or cancel the subscription.

---

## Opening the Billing Page

From anywhere in the application, click the **gear icon** (⚙️) in the top navigation bar to open workspace **Settings**. In the Settings sidebar, click **Billing**.

> **Note:** If your workspace role is **Member**, the Billing page shows the plan, Graphium credits, and plan resource usage only. **Change plan**, **Update payment method**, the **Billing portal** card, **Recent Invoices**, and **Cancel subscription** are hidden. Contact a workspace Owner or Admin for billing changes.

## Current Plan and Subscription Status

The top section of the Billing page displays your workspace's active subscription:

- **Plan name** — the name of the plan your workspace is currently on
- **Subscription status** — whether the subscription is active, in a trial period, or pending a change
- **Next invoice** — the upcoming invoice amount and the date it will be charged
- **Billing period** — the current billing cycle start and end dates

Owners and Admins can click **Change plan** to review available plans and upgrade or downgrade. See [Plan Comparison](/graphium/billing/subscription-and-payment-settings#plan-comparison) for what each plan includes. If the workspace has not yet started a paid subscription, this section explains the current free or trial state and provides an entry point to review available plans.

## Graphium Credits

Graphium Credits are used to power AI features within the workspace, such as AI Assistant conversations and AI-driven playbook actions. The **Graphium credits** section of the Billing page shows your workspace's total available credits, split into two pools:

- **Monthly credits** — included with your plan and reset every month
- **Purchased credits** — used after monthly credits are exhausted, and expire 365 days after purchase

If you need additional credits, click **Buy credits** to purchase more. Credits are shared across the workspace and are consumed by all vault activity that uses AI features.

## Credit Usage This Cycle

Below the total credit usage summary, the **Credit usage this cycle** section shows a breakdown of how the workspace's credits were spent across services during the current billing period. The breakdown table lists each service that consumed credits — such as AI assistant conversations and playbook actions — alongside the number of credits used.

This breakdown is useful for understanding which features are driving the most credit consumption before deciding whether to adjust usage or purchase additional credits.

## Plan Resource Usage

The **Plan resource usage** section of the Billing page shows current consumption against the limits included in your plan. Usage is displayed as "current of maximum" (for example, "1 of 3 vaults"), or as **Unlimited** when your plan has no cap for that resource.

Workspace-level limits track resources shared across the entire workspace:

- Vaults
- Active playbooks
- Connections

Per-vault limits are tracked separately for each vault and shown in a table below the workspace-level block:

- Custom tables per vault
- Records per vault
- Custom AI assistants per vault

If the workspace approaches or reaches a limit, Graphium AI displays an in-app prompt guiding you to the Billing page to review upgrade options. See [Plan Limits and Upgrade Guidance](/graphium/billing/subscription-and-payment-settings#plan-limits-and-upgrade-guidance) for more detail on how limit enforcement works.

## Payment and Invoice Management

This section is visible to workspace Owners and Admins only.

The right side of the Billing page includes a **Billing portal** card with an **Open billing portal** button. The card also displays connection status fields — including the subscription service, connection status, last sync time, and entitlement source — which reflect how your workspace's subscription data is synced with the billing provider. Click **Open billing portal** to manage your payment method, update subscription settings, and download receipts.

A **Recent Invoices** section also appears on the right side of the page, showing the most recent charges. Click **View all invoices** to open the full invoice history in the billing portal.

Both of these routes to the billing portal return you to Graphium AI once you are done.

## Permissions

| Action | Owner | Admin | Builder | Contributor |
|--------|-------|-------|---------|-------------|
| View the Billing page | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| View plan, credits, and limits | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Change plan or cancel the subscription | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Access payment and invoice management | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

> **Note:** Builder and Contributor here refer to workspace **Members** with that seat type. A Member's access to billing is view-only.
