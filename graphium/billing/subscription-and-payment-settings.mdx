## Overview

Workspace Owners and Admins can manage the workspace subscription directly from the **Billing** page — upgrading or changing the current plan, updating the payment method on file, and accessing invoice history. This article also explains how Graphium AI handles plan limits and what users see when the workspace approaches or reaches a limit.

---

## Upgrading or Changing Your Plan

To start a plan change, open the **Billing** page from workspace **Settings**. The Billing page displays the current plan alongside options to upgrade or change the subscription.

When you initiate a plan change:

1. Graphium AI shows a **Choose a paid plan** comparison — Starter, Core, Pro, and Enterprise — with per-seat pricing and what's included in each. Builder and Contributor seats are billed separately.
2. Select a plan and click **Continue with [Plan]**. You are redirected to **Stripe Checkout** to save a payment method and complete the upgrade.
3. Once the change is confirmed in Stripe, Graphium AI updates the workspace's subscription state and the Billing page reflects the new plan.

> **Note:** Plan changes that succeed are reflected on the Billing page immediately. No manual refresh is required.

For a side-by-side view of each plan's limits, see [Plan Comparison](#plan-comparison) below.

### Downgrading and AI models

The **Free** and **Starter** plans can use standard-tier AI models only; **Core** and **Pro** can use every model. If you move to a plan that doesn't include an assistant's selected model, the assistant keeps working: its chats, playbook steps, and sub-assistant calls run on the workspace default model instead. The assistant's saved model setting isn't changed, so it switches back automatically if you upgrade again. You can't save an assistant with a model above your plan's tier — those models show an **UPGRADE** label in the model picker.

- Every vault also includes the **General Assistant**, which doesn't count toward the custom assistant limit.
- **Connections** and **Active playbooks** are counted across the whole workspace; tables, records, and custom assistants are counted per vault.

- For pricing, click **Change plan** on the Billing page. For larger teams or custom limits, ask about the **Enterprise** plan — see [How to Contact Support or Sales](/graphium/reference/how-to-contact-support-or-sales).

## Managing Your Payment Method and Invoices

Payment method management and invoice history are both accessed through the **Billing portal** — a Stripe-managed interface linked from the Billing page.

To open the billing portal:

1. Open **Settings** → **Billing**.
2. In the **Billing portal** card on the right side of the page, click **Open billing portal**.
3. The Stripe billing portal opens, where you can update your payment method, download receipts, and manage subscription settings.
4. Return to Graphium AI once you are done.

To go directly to your invoice history, click **View all invoices** in the **Recent Invoices** card on the right side of the Billing page. This also opens the Stripe billing portal, navigating to the invoice list.

The payment method on file is used for recurring subscription charges and for seat capacity increases initiated from the Billing page.

## Plan Limits and Upgrade Guidance

Each plan includes workspace-level resource limits — such as the maximum number of vaults, tables, records, active playbooks, and AI assistants. Graphium AI enforces these limits and provides in-app guidance when the workspace approaches or reaches them.

### Approaching a limit

When the workspace is close to a plan limit, Graphium AI surfaces a proactive warning within the relevant area of the product — for example, near the vault list when the workspace is approaching its vault limit. The warning tells you which resource is near capacity so you can plan ahead.

### Reaching a limit

When an action would exceed a plan limit, Graphium AI blocks the action and displays a clear message that identifies the specific limit reached. The message includes a prompt to navigate to the **Billing** page to review the current plan and available upgrade options.

Limits enforced this way include:

Workspace-level limits:
- Total vault count
- Active playbooks
- Connections

Per-vault limits:
- Custom tables per vault
- Records per vault
- Custom AI assistants per vault

On paid plans, custom AI assistants and connections are **Unlimited**, so you won't see a limit prompt for them. See [Plan Comparison](#plan-comparison) for each plan's numbers.

> **Note:** Limit enforcement messages identify the specific resource that was blocked — you will not see a generic error. This makes it easier to understand what needs to change before the action can complete.

### Acting on a limit prompt

If you see a limit prompt, click the link in the message to go directly to the Billing page. From there, you can review your current plan entitlements and start an upgrade if you need additional capacity.

## Canceling Your Subscription

The **Cancel subscription** section near the bottom of the Billing page lets an Owner or Admin cancel the workspace's plan. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, then cancels automatically.

1. In Workspace Settings, click **Billing**.
2. Scroll to **Cancel subscription** and click **Cancel subscription**, then follow the prompts to confirm.
