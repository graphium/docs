## Overview

A workspace in Graphium AI is the top-level organizational container for your vaults, data, and team members. If your account belongs to multiple workspaces — for example, separate workspaces for different practices or facilities — you can identify your active workspace, view all available workspaces, and switch between them at any time from the navigation bar.

---

## The Workspace Indicator

The workspace indicator appears in the **top-left corner of the navigation bar**, immediately to the right of the Graphium logo. It consists of:

- A **colored square icon** displaying the workspace's two-letter abbreviation (e.g., **UA** for Urban Anesthesia)
- The **full workspace name** in white text
- A **chevron arrow (∨)** indicating the name is clickable
- A **gear icon (⚙)** to the right for accessing workspace settings

The color of the icon is unique to each workspace, making it easy to identify your active workspace at a glance.

## Viewing Your Available Workspaces

1. From any page in Graphium AI, click the **workspace name** or **chevron arrow (∨)** in the top-left of the navigation bar.

2. The dropdown lists all workspaces available to your account, each showing a colored icon and full workspace name. Your current workspace is highlighted in the list.

3. A **Manage workspaces** link appears at the bottom of the list.

4. To close without switching, press **Escape** or click anywhere outside the dropdown.

---

## Switching Workspaces

1. Open the workspace switcher by clicking the workspace name or chevron arrow (∨).

2. Click the name of the workspace you want to switch to.

3. The page refreshes and the nav bar updates to the new workspace's icon and name.

When you switch, the workspace home reloads showing only that workspace's assistants (on the **Assistants** tab) and vaults (on the **Vaults** tab). Switching does **not** log you out, affect your session, delete any data, or change your user role. Roles are set per workspace.

> **Note:** Any user belonging to more than one workspace can switch freely — no special role is required. Creating or deactivating workspaces is restricted to **Workspace Owners**.

---

## The Workspaces Tab

Clicking **Manage workspaces** at the bottom of the dropdown opens the **Workspaces** tab of Workspace Settings. It lists all your workspaces, with an **Add a workspace** button (Workspace Owners only) and a **Switch to this workspace** button for every workspace other than your current one, which is labeled *You are currently in this workspace*.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely Cause | What to Do |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| Workspace indicator is not visible | Navigation bar failed to load | Refresh the page; check your internet connection |
| Workspace shown is not the one I expected | You may be in a different workspace | Click the indicator to open the switcher and select the correct workspace |
| A workspace I don't recognize is shown | Account was added to a workspace by an administrator | Contact your workspace administrator |
| Only one workspace appears in the dropdown | Account may only belong to one workspace | Contact your administrator if you believe you need access to additional workspaces |
| The workspace I need is not in the list | You haven't been added to that workspace yet | Contact your administrator to request access |
| Switcher does not respond when clicked | Page or browser issue | Refresh the page and try again |
| The Vaults tab shows "No vaults yet" after switching | The selected workspace has no vaults | Expected for a new workspace — create a vault from this screen |
| Accidentally switched to the wrong workspace | — | Open the switcher again and click the correct workspace |
