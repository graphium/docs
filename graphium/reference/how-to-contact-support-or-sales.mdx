## Overview

The Help Assistant panel includes two options for getting in touch with the Graphium AI team directly — **Submit a ticket** for support issues, and **Talk to sales** for questions about plans, pricing, or expanding your workspace. Both are accessible from the **?** icon in the top navigation bar, available from anywhere in the app.

## When to Use This

- Use **Submit a ticket** when you're experiencing a technical issue, unexpected behavior, or need help with something the Help Assistant couldn't resolve.
- Use **Talk to sales** when you have questions about pricing, adding seats, upgrading your plan, or exploring what Graphium AI can do for your organization.

## Prerequisites

- You must be signed in to your Graphium AI workspace.
- No special role or access level is required — all users can submit a ticket or contact sales.

---

## Submit a Support Ticket

1. Click the **?** icon in the top navigation bar to open the Help panel.
2. Click **Submit a ticket** at the bottom of the panel.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/reference/help-panel-home.png" alt="Help panel showing Submit a ticket and Talk to sales buttons" />
</Frame>

3. In the **Subject** field, enter a brief summary of the issue (e.g., "Connection not syncing" or "Unable to publish playbook").
4. In the **Details** field, describe what happened and what you expected instead.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/reference/submit-ticket-form.png" alt="Submit a support ticket form" />
</Frame>

5. Click **Submit**.
6. A confirmation screen appears with a unique ticket number (e.g., **#119**). Keep this number for your records — you can reference it when following up with the support team. The Graphium AI support team will follow up by email.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/reference/submit-ticket-confirmation.png" alt="Support ticket confirmation screen showing ticket number" />
</Frame>

> **Note:** Do not include PHI, patient information, or personal identifiers in your ticket.

---

## Talk to Sales

1. Click the **?** icon in the top navigation bar to open the Help panel.
2. Click **Talk to sales** at the bottom of the panel.
3. In the **Subject** field, enter a brief summary of what you're looking for (e.g., "Interested in adding more users" or "Question about enterprise pricing").
4. In the **Details** field, provide any additional context about your needs.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/reference/talk-to-sales-form.png" alt="Talk to sales form" />
</Frame>

5. Click **Submit**.
6. A confirmation screen appears with a unique ticket number (e.g., **#120**). A member of the Graphium AI sales team will be in touch shortly.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/graphium/reference/talk-to-sales-confirmation.png" alt="Talk to sales confirmation screen showing ticket number" />
</Frame>

---

## What to Expect

After submitting either form, you'll see a confirmation screen displaying a unique ticket number. For support tickets, the Graphium AI support team will follow up by email. For sales inquiries, a member of the sales team will reach out directly. Response times may vary.

## Common Issues & Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Submit button is unresponsive | Subject or Details field may be empty | Ensure both fields are filled in before submitting |
| Confirmation screen did not appear | Browser or connection issue | Refresh the page, reopen the Help panel, and try submitting again |
| Did not receive a follow-up email after submitting a ticket | Email may have been filtered | Check your spam or junk folder; if no email arrives, submit a new ticket |
