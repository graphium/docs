## Overview

Graphium AI lets you personalize each vault's identity by editing its name, adding a description, and choosing a custom icon and color. These settings help your team quickly identify vaults at a glance and keep the workspace organized.

The same **General** settings page also lets Admins and Builders control whether AI assistants and playbooks can modify the vault's structure, and lets Workspace Owners deactivate or delete the vault entirely.

## Prerequisites

- You must have an existing vault in Graphium AI
- You must have the **Admin** or **Builder** role in that vault

> **Note:** Contributor and Viewer roles can view the vault name, icon, and color, but cannot make changes.

## Editing the Vault Name

The vault name can be updated in two ways: directly from the vault header (quick inline edit) or from the General Settings page.

### Option A: Inline Edit from the Vault Header

1. Open the vault you want to rename.
2. In the vault header at the top of the page, locate the vault name next to the vault icon.
3. Click the **pencil icon (✏️)** to the right of the vault name.
4. The name becomes an active text input with a cursor. Edit the name as needed.

> 💡 **Tip:** The vault name must not be empty and has a maximum length of 255 characters. Leading and trailing spaces are automatically trimmed.

5. Press **Enter** to save the new name. The header updates immediately.
6. Press **Escape** to cancel and revert to the original name without saving.

### Option B: Edit from Vault Settings

1. Open the vault and click **Settings** in the vault sidebar.
2. On the **General** page, locate the **Vault details** card.
3. Click into the **Vault Name** field and update the name.
4. Click away (blur the field) or navigate away to save. The change is applied automatically.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you clear the Vault Name field entirely, the save will be blocked — the name is required. An inline error will appear to prompt you to enter a value.

## Editing the Vault Description

The vault description is optional and is managed from the vault's General settings page.

1. Open the vault and click **Settings** in the vault sidebar.
2. On the **General** page, locate the **Vault details** card.
3. Click into the **Description** text area and type or update the description.

> 💡 **Tip:** Descriptions have a maximum length of 500 characters. A good description helps new team members quickly understand what data or workflows a vault contains.

4. Click away (blur the field) to save. The description is saved automatically.
5. To **remove the description entirely**, clear the text area and click away. The field saves as empty (null), effectively clearing it.

## Selecting a Vault Icon & Color

The vault icon and color are set from the same **Vault details** card as the name and description, and help visually distinguish vaults on the dashboard.

1. Open the vault and click **Settings** in the vault sidebar.
2. On the **General** page, scroll down within the **Vault details** card to the **Vault Icon** and **Vault Color** sections.
3. In the **Vault Icon** section, click any of the 7 available icons to select it.
4. In the **Vault Color** section, click any of the 6 available colors — the selected color gets a border ring, and the vault icon updates immediately to use that color as its background.

Changes save automatically as soon as you click an icon or color — no Save button is required, and there is no popover to confirm or dismiss.

## Assistant Table and Column Changes

Further down the **General** page, the **Assistant table and column changes** section controls what AI assistants and playbooks are allowed to change about this vault's tables. Both settings are off by default.

- **Let assistants add and edit columns** — when off, assistants and playbooks cannot add or edit columns anywhere in this vault, regardless of what any individual table's own settings say. Those per-table settings are preserved and take effect again as soon as you turn this on.
- **Let assistants create new tables** — when off, assistants and playbooks cannot create new tables in this vault. They can still add or edit columns on tables that already exist, if the setting above allows it.

Both toggles save immediately — no Save button is required.

> 💡 **Tip:** This vault-level setting is what makes the **Allow column changes** toggle on an individual assistant's Tools tab available or greyed out. See [Creating and Updating Records with the AI Assistant](/graphium/ai-assistants/creating-and-updating-records-with-the-ai-assistant) for how it applies to a specific assistant.

## Deactivating or Deleting a Vault

At the bottom of the **General** page, the **Danger Zone** lets Workspace Owners deactivate or delete the vault. This is a Workspace Owner–only control and is covered in full in [Deleting or Deactivating a Vault](/graphium/vaults/deleting-or-deactivating-a-vault).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Pencil icon is not visible next to the vault name | You have a Contributor or Viewer role | Contact your vault ADMIN to request a higher role |
| Vault icon is not clickable | You have a Contributor or Viewer role | Contact your vault ADMIN to request a higher role |
| Name change reverts immediately after saving | API error or validation failure | Check that the name is not empty and is under 255 characters. Try again — if it persists, check your network connection |
| Vault name field won't save when empty | Empty names are not allowed | Enter at least one character before saving |
| Description is not saving | Possible network issue | Ensure you clicked away from the text area to trigger the save. Reload the page and try again |
| "Insufficient permissions" error | Your vault role is Contributor or Viewer | Contact your vault ADMIN or Workspace Owner to adjust your permissions |
