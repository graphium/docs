## Overview

Every user with vault access is assigned one of four roles that controls what they can see and do. Vault Admins manage access from the **Access & Permissions** page in vault Settings — adding workspace members to the vault, assigning and changing roles, and deactivating users when needed.

---

## Vault Roles

| Role | What They Can Do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Admin** | Full access to manage the vault, its users, and all data. Can change vault settings, add and remove users, change roles, create and modify tables, forms, and workflows, and add and edit records. |
| **Builder** | Can create and modify tables, forms, and workflows. Can add and edit records but cannot manage vault users or settings. |
| **Contributor** | Can add and edit data records but cannot modify vault structure. Cannot access vault settings or user management. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access. Can view records and data but cannot add, edit, or delete anything. |

The same summary appears in the **Role Permissions** card at the bottom of the Access & Permissions page.

---

## Opening Access & Permissions

1. Open the vault and click **Settings** in the **VAULT** section of the sidebar.
2. Click **Access & Permissions** (*People and access*) in the settings menu.

The page has three parts: **Add Member** at the top, **People with Vault Access** below it, and the **Role Permissions** card at the bottom.

---

## Adding a Vault User

You can only add people who are already members of the workspace. To invite someone new, add them to the workspace first — see [Managing Workspace Members](/graphium/getting-started/managing-workspace-members) — then add them to the vault here.

1. In the **Add Member** section, search for the workspace member by name or email. Workspace members who aren't in the vault yet are listed below the search box.
2. Select the member.
3. Choose their vault role from the dropdown next to the search box (defaults to **Contributor**).
4. Click **Add Member**.

The person is added immediately and can access the vault on their next visit. No email notification is sent.

## Viewing Vault Users

The **People with Vault Access** list shows how many active members the vault has. Each row shows the user's avatar, full name, email, their vault role, and a **Deactivate** link.

## Changing a User's Vault Role

1. In **People with Vault Access**, find the user.
2. Click the **role dropdown** on their row and select the new role.

The change takes effect immediately with no notification sent.

> **Note:** A vault must always have at least one Admin. If you are the only Admin, you cannot change your own role to a lower level.

## Deactivating a Vault User

1. In **People with Vault Access**, find the user.
2. Click **Deactivate** on their row.
3. Confirm the action in the confirmation dialog that appears.

The user's vault access is immediately suspended. Their Graphium AI account remains active and they keep access to any other vaults they belong to. Any records they created remain intact. To restore access, add them again from **Add Member** with the appropriate role.

## Permissions

| Action | Admin | Builder | Contributor | Viewer |
|--------|-------|---------|-------------|--------|
| View Access & Permissions page | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

| Remove a user from the vault | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely Cause | What to Do |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| Access & Permissions page is not visible | You don't have Admin or Builder role | Contact your vault Admin |
| A person doesn't appear in the Add Member search | They aren't a workspace member yet, or they're already in the vault | Invite them to the workspace first via [Managing Workspace Members](/graphium/getting-started/managing-workspace-members), or check People with Vault Access |
| The Add Member section is not visible | You don't have the Admin role | Only vault Admins can add users |
| User was added but can't access the vault | They may need to refresh | Ask the user to refresh their browser or sign in again |
| Role dropdown is greyed out | You don't have the Admin role | Only Admins can change roles |
| Accidentally deactivated a user | — | Add them again from **Add Member** with the appropriate role |
