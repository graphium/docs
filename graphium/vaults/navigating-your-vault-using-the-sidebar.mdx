## Overview

The vault sidebar is the primary navigation panel within a vault. It runs along the left side of the screen and gives you access to every part of your vault from one place: the Home page, your assistants and chats, your tables, and the vault's Playbooks, Connections, and Settings.

The sidebar also shows your current location — the active assistant, chat, table, or page is highlighted in teal so you always know where you are.

---

## Sidebar Layout

From top to bottom, the vault sidebar contains:

**Home**
A link (house icon) to the vault's Home page, which contains the AI chat interface, recent conversations, and suggested prompts.

**ASSISTANTS**
The **General Assistant** first, followed by the vault's published custom assistants. If there are more than five, a **"X More"** control expands the list. Click an assistant to start a new chat with it. Vault Admins also see two buttons in the section header:

- **⚙ (Manage assistants)** — opens the Custom Assistants page, where you can create, edit, and archive assistants
- **+ (Add assistant)** — creates a new assistant

Builders don't see these buttons. They open the Custom Assistants page from **Manage assistants** at the bottom of the assistant selector on the Home page.

**CHATS**
Your recent conversations in this vault. Each row shows the icon of the assistant the chat was held with. A **+** button starts a new General Assistant chat, and **"X More"** expands the list. Chats with an archived assistant are labeled **Inactive**.

**TABLES**
All your vault's tables. Each row shows a table icon, the table name, and — when the table contains records — a badge with the table's total record count. A **+** button in the section header (visible to Admin and Builder users) lets you add a new table. **"X More"** expands the full list, and **"Show Less"** collapses it again.

**VAULT**
Links to the vault's **Playbooks** (with a badge showing the number of playbooks), **Connections** (with a badge showing the number of connections), and **Settings**.

**Builder / Contributor Mode Toggle**
At the bottom of the sidebar, Admin and Builder users see a **Builder / Contributor** toggle that switches the sidebar between the full builder view and the contributor view.

**Collapse Button**
An icon-only button at the very bottom of the sidebar collapses it to a narrow icon-only rail, hiding all text labels. The collapsed rail doesn't show your chats.

## Navigating to Home

1. Click **Home** at the top of the sidebar (below the vault header).
2. The Home page loads in the main content area, showing the chat input, suggested prompts, and recent conversations.

Home is visible to all vault roles: Admin, Builder, Contributor, and Viewer.

## Navigating to an Assistant

1. In the **ASSISTANTS** section, click the assistant you want to chat with. If it isn't visible, click **"X More"**.
2. A new chat with that assistant opens on the Home page.

To create, edit, or archive assistants, Admins click the **⚙** in the ASSISTANTS header, and Builders click **Manage assistants** at the bottom of the assistant selector. See [Managing Your AI Assistants](/graphium/ai-assistants/managing-your-ai-assistants) and [The AI Assistant Home Page](/graphium/ai-assistants/the-ai-assistant-welcome-screen#choosing-an-assistant).

---

## Navigating to a Chat

1. In the **CHATS** section, find the conversation you want to reopen. If it isn't visible, click **"X More"**.
2. Click the chat. The conversation opens so you can continue it.

A chat labeled **Inactive** was held with an assistant that has since been archived. You can read it but not continue it. See [Chats with an Archived Assistant](/graphium/ai-assistants/the-ai-assistant-welcome-screen#chats-with-an-archived-assistant).

---

## Navigating to a Table

1. In the **TABLES** section, find the table you want to open.
2. If the table isn't visible, click **"X More"** to expand the full list.
3. Click the table name. The table's Data view loads in the main content area, and the table is highlighted in the sidebar to show it's active.

> **Note:** Admin and Builder users see all tables (including Draft tables). Contributor and Viewer users see only tables that have been enabled for Contributor Mode.

## Navigating to Playbooks, Connections, and Settings

In the **VAULT** section at the bottom of the sidebar, click:

- **Playbooks** — opens the Playbooks page. See [Managing Your Playbooks](/graphium/playbooks/managing-your-playbooks).
- **Connections** — opens the Connections page. See [Working with a Connection](/graphium/connections/working-with-a-connection).
- **Settings** — opens Vault Settings. See [Vault Settings](/graphium/vaults/vault-settings-general-configuration).

If your chat or table list is long, scroll down within the sidebar to reach the VAULT section.

> **Note:** The VAULT section is only visible to **Admin** and **Builder** users.

## Returning to the Workspace Home

Click the **back arrow (←)** in the top-left corner of the vault header to return to the workspace home, which opens on the **Assistants** tab. See [The AI Assistant Home Page](/graphium/ai-assistants/the-ai-assistant-welcome-screen#starting-from-the-workspace-home).

## Permissions

What you see in the sidebar depends on your vault role:

| Sidebar Item | Admin | Builder | Contributor | Viewer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Assistants | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Manage (⚙) and Add (+) assistant buttons | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| CHATS list in the sidebar | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Tables (all) | ✅ | ✅ | — | — |
| Tables (contributor-enabled only) | — | — | ✅ | ✅ (read-only) |
| Add table (+) | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Vault section (Playbooks, Connections, Settings) | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Builder/Contributor mode toggle | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

> **Note:** Every vault role can chat with the vault's assistants. Contributors and Viewers don't see the **CHATS** list in the sidebar, but they can reopen recent conversations from **Recent Chats** on the Home page. Each custom assistant's **Who Can Access** setting controls which roles see it in the ASSISTANTS section.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely Cause | What to Do |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| Playbooks, Connections, or Settings not visible | Your vault role is Contributor or Viewer, or a long list is pushing the VAULT section out of view | Scroll down within the sidebar. If it still isn't there, contact your vault Admin — this section requires Admin or Builder access |
| The ⚙ or + next to ASSISTANTS is missing | These buttons are for vault Admins only | Builders can use **Manage assistants** at the bottom of the assistant selector. Contributors and Viewers can't manage assistants — contact your vault Admin |
| An assistant you expect isn't listed | It isn't published, has been archived, or isn't available to your role | Ask a vault Admin to check the assistant's status and **Who Can Access** setting |
| A table you expect is not listed | The table may not be enabled for Contributor Mode | If you are a Contributor or Viewer, ask an Admin or Builder to enable that table for Contributor Mode |
| The sidebar is showing only icons | The sidebar has been collapsed | Click the **Expand** button at the bottom of the icon bar to restore the full sidebar |
| My chats don't appear in the sidebar | The sidebar is collapsed, you're in the contributor view, or your vault role is Contributor or Viewer | Expand the sidebar, or switch the toggle at the bottom back to **Builder**. Contributors and Viewers can reopen chats from **Recent Chats** on the Home page |
| Clicking a sidebar item does nothing | The destination page may still be loading | Wait a moment and try again; check your network connection if the issue persists |
