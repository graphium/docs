## Overview

The **Vaults** tab of the workspace home lists every vault you have access to, as either a grid of cards or a list of rows. From here you can browse your vaults and open any one to access its assistants, chats, tables, playbooks, connections, and settings.

> **Note:** The workspace home opens on the **Assistants** tab. Click **Vaults** next to it to see your vaults. See [Browsing Assistants in Your Workspace](/graphium/ai-assistants/browsing-assistants-in-your-workspace).

---

## The Vaults Tab

The tab is headed **Your Vaults**. By default, vaults are displayed as cards in a responsive grid. Each card gives you a quick summary before you open the vault:

- **Vault icon** — displayed in the upper-left corner using the icon and color assigned to that vault
- **Vault name** — shown in bold below the icon
- **Record count** — total number of records across all tables in the vault
- **Table count** — total number of tables in the vault
- **Last Updated Timestamp** — date the vault was last modified

The first card in the grid is always the **Create a Vault** card (dashed border, **+** icon). Clicking it opens the vault creation flow.

## Switching Between Grid and List View

Use the view toggle icons in the upper-right corner of the Vaults tab:

- **Grid icon (⊞)** — displays vaults as cards with names, record counts, and table counts
- **List icon (☰)** — displays vaults as rows with Name, Last Updated, Records and Tables columns

Your view preference is remembered across sessions.

## Opening a Vault

Click anywhere on a vault card or row to open it. The vault opens on the **Home** page, where you can chat with the vault's AI assistants, view recent conversations, and see suggested prompts based on your data.

Each time you update a vault, the **Last Updated** timestamp updates automatically. Only vaults you have been granted access to appear on the Vaults tab. To include deactivated vaults, turn on **Show inactive** in the upper-right corner.

---

## What You See When a Vault Opens

When you open a vault you are taken to the vault interior, which includes:

- A **vault header** at the top showing the vault name, record count, and last updated date
- A **sidebar** on the left with Home, Assistants, Chats, Tables, and the Vault section (Playbooks, Connections, and Settings) — see [Navigating Your Vault](/graphium/vaults/navigating-your-vault-using-the-sidebar)
- The **Home** page, with the General Assistant selected, as the default landing point

To return to the workspace home, click the **back arrow (←)** in the top-left corner of the vault header. The workspace home opens on the **Assistants** tab.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely Cause | What to Do |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| I only see assistants, not vaults | The workspace home opens on the **Assistants** tab | Click the **Vaults** tab |
| A vault is missing from the Vaults tab | You haven't been added to that vault, or it is inactive | Turn on **Show inactive**, or contact your workspace administrator to request access |
| The grid shows fewer vaults than expected | You may be in the wrong workspace | Check the workspace name in the top navigation bar |
| Clicking a vault card does nothing | A temporary UI issue | Refresh the page and try again |
| I want to open a table, not the Home page | Vaults always open on the Home page | Click the table under **TABLES** in the sidebar |
| The Vaults tab shows "No vaults yet" | No vaults have been created in this workspace | Use the **Create a Vault** card to create one, or ask an Admin |
